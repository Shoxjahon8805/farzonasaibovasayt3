A short tulip story

Born in the mountains

Wild tulips first grew in the mountains and plains of Central Asia, including areas of Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan and Turkey.

Loved by sultans

In the Ottoman Empire, tulips were a symbol of beauty and were grown in royal gardens.

Famous in Holland

Tulips reached the Netherlands in the 1500s. Soon they were so popular that bulbs sold for huge prices. Today the Dutch grow billions of them.

How a tulip grows

  1. Plant the bulb in autumn. Put it pointy side up in the soil, about 15 cm deep.
  2. Rest in the cold. Winter's chill tells the bulb it is time to prepare for spring.
  3. Shoots appear. When the soil warms, green leaves push up through the ground.
  4. The flower blooms. A bright cup opens on top of the stem and bees come to visit.
  5. The bulb recharges. After blooming, leaves feed the bulb so it can flower again next year.
